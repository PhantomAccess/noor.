Terms of Use

1. Location Usage

This app may use your location to calculate prayer times and Qiblah direction. Location is only used when necessary and with your permission.

2. Accuracy Disclaimer

Prayer times and Qiblah direction are calculated using standard methods and device sensors. Results may vary. Always verify with a trusted local source when precision matters.

3. Offline Use

After initial setup, Quran reading works offline. Prayer times and Qiblah still require location access.

4. Quran Content

Quran text and translations are provided for personal reading and reflection. We do not claim ownership of the sacred text.
